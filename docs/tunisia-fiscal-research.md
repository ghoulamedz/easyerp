# Tunisia fiscal compliance check — easyERP (best effort, 2026-10-13)

> Accounting is not our business. This is a best-effort engineering brief from public web sources (mostly Tunisian practitioner blogs + official pages), not legal advice. Freshness matters: loi de finances 2026 and note commune 02/2026 are in flux — confirm with an expert-comptable before building.

## 1. Invoice mentions (art. 18-II TVA) — verdict: mostly COMPLIANT, gaps on conditional lines
- **Claim:** Art. 18-II requires: date, client name+address (+client MF if declarant), seller MF, description + HT price, VAT rates + amounts; art. 18-III requires numbering in an unbroken series. **Sources:** [Gesty facture conforme](https://gesty.tn/blog/facture-conforme-loi-tunisie-mentions-obligatoires), [Jurisite art. 18](https://www.jurisitetunisie.com/tunisie/codes/tva/tva1060.htm). **Support:** direct evidence. **Confidence:** high.
- easyERP already carries: locale, VAT regime, per-rate tax groups, MF, timbre kept out of VAT base, retenue — matches the core checklist. **Inference:** compliant on the mandatory core.
- **Gaps:** (a) FODEC line when applicable; (b) exemption/suspension mention + attestation ref + suspended-VAT amount; (c) "droit de timbre payé sur déclaration" mention; (d) client-MF-required logic; (e) amount in words (usage, but expected on PDFs). See gap list.
- **ICE:** no Tunisian "ICE" found — ICE is the Moroccan identifier. Tunisia's identifier is the **matricule fiscal** (7 digits + key + TVA code + category + establishment, e.g. 1234567X/A/M/000; 13 chars without slashes in TEIF). **Inference, confidence:** medium — flag any "ICE" field as Morocco-specific, do not add it.

## 2. VAT rates, timbre, retenue, FODEC — verdict: RATES COMPLIANT, CALCULATION ORDER GAPS
- **Claim:** Rates in force are 0/7/13/19% (19% standard); money to 3 decimals (millimes). **Sources:** [Gesty TVA calc](https://gesty.tn/outils/calcul-tva), [IntegraSys TVA 2026](https://integrasys-erp.com/ressources/fiscalite-tunisienne/tva-tunisie-guide). **Support:** direct evidence. **Confidence:** high. Mixed-rate documents with per-rate breakdown are normal (Gesty's own example mixes 19% + 7%) — easyERP's `document_tax_group` model is **compliant**.
- **Claim:** Timbre = 1.000 DT per facture, added after TTC, never in VAT base; LF 2026 adds 1.500/2.000 DT tiers for grandes surfaces (>3000m² built / >1500m² sales area); exemptions include export. **Sources:** [Gesty timbre 2026](https://gesty.tn/blog/timbre-fiscal-facture-tunisie) (cites CDET art. 117–127, LF 2026 art. 20). **Support:** direct evidence. **Confidence:** high. easyERP "timbre out of VAT base" is **compliant**; missing tiers/exemptions = gap.
- **Claim:** Order of calculation: remise → FODEC 1% on HT net (line-level) → VAT base = HT + FODEC → TVA → TTC → timbre → retenue on TTC (hors timbre en pratique, note commune 12/2015) → net à payer. **Sources:** [eFactureTN calcul](https://efacturetn.com/fr/blog/timbre-fiscal-fodec-retenue-source-facture-tunisie), [Gesty timbre](https://gesty.tn/blog/timbre-fiscal-facture-tunisie). **Support:** direct evidence. **Confidence:** medium-high (retenue-hors-timbre has no express text for invoice stamp — flagged as practice, not statute).
- **Claim:** Retenue is the *client's* withholding (seuil 1 000 DT TTC; 1%/0.5%/1.5% on goods/services by supplier regime; 3% honoraires réel, etc.), evidenced by a TEJ certificate the supplier imputes. **Source:** [Gesty facture conforme](https://gesty.tn/blog/facture-conforme-loi-tunisie-mentions-obligatoires) + [eFactureTN](https://efacturetn.com/fr/blog/timbre-fiscal-fodec-retenue-source-facture-tunisie). **Confidence:** medium. Implication: easyERP should *display* RAS + net à payer and track certificates, never withhold itself on sales.

## 3. Numbering + immutability — verdict: COMPLIANT
- **Claim:** Numbers must be unique, chronological, continuous, no gaps; deleted/cancelled invoice corrected only by avoir; sanctions art. 95–96 CDPF (250–10 000 DT for missing mentions; 50–1 000 DT per invoice for bad series). **Sources:** [Gesty numérotation](https://gesty.tn/blog/numerotation-sequentielle-factures-tunisie), [facturetunisie.pro](https://facturetunisie.pro/numerotation-facture-tunisie), [Gesty conforme sanctions](https://gesty.tn/blog/facture-conforme-loi-tunisie-mentions-obligatoires). **Support:** direct evidence. **Confidence:** high.
- easyERP (gapless per-type-per-year series via atomic UPDATE…RETURNING, DB triggers, no delete route for Issued, correction only by credit note) matches exactly. Per-year reset with prefix (FAC-2026-…) is the local convention. **Keep cancelled orders numbered** (already the design) — deleting them would open holes.

## 4. Avoirs — verdict: COMPLIANT design, two UX gaps
- **Claim:** No avoir-specific article; practice applies art. 18 mentions + "Avoir" title, own continuous series (e.g. AV-2026-…), original invoice ref + motive; seller imputes VAT per art. 9-IV-5°. **Source:** [Gesty avoir](https://gesty.tn/blog/facture-avoir-tunisie). **Support:** direct evidence. **Confidence:** high.
- easyERP (own AV series, prices from stored invoice figures, over-credit refused, immutable) is **compliant** — pricing from the invoice rather than current catalog is exactly right.
- **Gaps:** (a) no timbre on the avoir itself is one practitioner's choice, but texts are silent — make it configurable, not hard-coded; (b) "credit note never moves stock" is legally fine (stock ≠ fiscal correction) but Tunisian practice expects the return recorded alongside — easyERP's separate RETURN_IN is acceptable **only if** the UI forces/links it on return-type avoirs.

## 5. E-invoicing / El Fatoora (TTN) — verdict: RISK (adapter is no-op while scope widens)
- **Claim:** El Fatoora = TEIF XML (UN/EDIFACT INVOIC), qualified signature (TunTrust), TTN validation → unique ref + QR + TTN signature; covers facture, avoir (code I-12), note d'honoraires; paper PDF-by-email ≠ e-invoice. **Source:** [Gesty El Fatoora](https://gesty.tn/blog/el-fatoora-facturation-electronique-tunisie). **Confidence:** high.
- **Claim:** Mandatory since 2016 for DGE↔public sector + drugs/fuel B2B; LF 2026 art. 53 extends to **services from 01/01/2026**, implemented progressively via note commune 02/2026 (transitional paper tolerated for filed-but-incomplete adhesions); paper-instead-of-electronic fine 100–500 DT/invoice ≤50 000 DT; April-2026 repeal/deferral proposal **not voted** as of 05/10/2026. **Sources:** [Zaatir 2026](https://cabinetzaatir.com/uncategorized/facturation-electronique-obligatoire-tunisie-2026/), [Jibaya avis DGI](https://jibaya.tn/blog/avis-aux-contribuables-concernes-par-la-facturation-electronique/), [Gesty El Fatoora](https://gesty.tn/blog/el-fatoora-facturation-electronique-tunisie). **Confidence:** medium-high (date-sensitive).
- easyERP's `ISSUED · TRANSMISSION_FAILED` + retry-never-renumber + stored TEIF fields is the **right architecture**, but a no-op adapter means any customer in scope must double-enter on TTN portal. Also: e-invoice does **not** remove timbre (TEIF code I-1601); transport still needs a stamped paper copy (20%-of-value fine otherwise). Both need building.

## 6. Archiving — verdict: DURATION COMPLIANT, PROBATIVE-VALUE RISK
- **Claim:** 10-year retention for books, invoices (issued + received), supporting docs (loi 96-112 art. 25); e-invoices archived per decree 2016-1066 incl. TTN-side copy. **Sources:** [COMPTA.tn](https://compta.tn/blog/obligations-comptables-pme-tunisie/), [Gesty El Fatoora archivage](https://gesty.tn/blog/el-fatoora-facturation-electronique-tunisie). **Confidence:** high.
- easyERP's PDF+SHA-256 archive matches the duration, but "host disk, owner's download job, excluded from DB backup" is the weak point: a probative archive must guarantee readability/consultation for 10 years (decree 2016-1066 cahier des charges). Disk-without-backup + no export tooling = loss/theft/corruption risk. Needs an export + integrity-check story.

## Prioritized gaps → concrete app changes
1. **P1 — FODEC support:** per-Item `fodecSubject` flag; pricing inserts FODEC line before VAT (base = HT net + FODEC, line-level); PDF + TEIF lines; avoir reprend FODEC. Without it, industrial-goods invoices are wrong.
2. **P1 — El Fatoora transmission adapter:** implement TEIF emission + ref/QR storage on the document; surface "paper copy with TTN ref + stamp" for goods transport; keep timbre on e-invoices. No-op is the biggest scope risk given 2026 services extension.
3. **P2 — Timbre rules:** configurable amount (1.000 default; 1.500/2.000 grandes-surfaces tiers); exemption flag (export, EPA mandates); print "droit de timbre payé sur déclaration" + auth ref; keep out of VAT *and* RAS bases; decide timbre-on-avoir via setting (default off), never silently.
4. **P2 — EXEMPT/suspension handling:** regime fields — mention text, attestation number+date, suspended-VAT amount; block EXEMPT without attestation ref (warning or hard block per accountant).
5. **P2 — Retenue display + certificates:** invoice footer shows RAS base (TTC hors timbre), rate, net à payer; store client RAS certificates against invoices; reduce Outstanding only on certificate, not on Hoffnung.
6. **P3 — MF discipline:** validate MF format at Party creation (incl. 13-char TEIF form); require client MF when client is declarant; support CIN for particuliers.
7. **P3 — Archive durability:** one-click fiscal-year export (PDFs + hashes + index), scheduled integrity check, documented restore; never tie archive lifetime to container disk.
8. **P3 — Re-pricing audit:** re-pricing at issue is *not* a fiscal violation per se, but an invoice re-priced from today's catalog instead of the agreed order/BL price creates commercial disputes. Record price source per line (catalog vs order vs manual) and, on return-type avoirs, require/link the RETURN_IN entry.
9. **Explicit non-goal:** do not add an "ICE" field — Moroccan concept; Tunisia = MF (+ RNE extract for onboarding, never on the facture).

## Contradictions / uncertainty
- Retenue base "hors timbre": consistent across practitioner sources, but admitted to lack express statutory text — medium confidence.
- Avoir timbré ou non: sources disagree / defer to accountant — left as a setting.
- LF 2026 services e-invoicing calendar and the April-2026 deferral proposal: time-sensitive; re-check JORT/note commune before committing roadmap.
- No primary-code verification done (CDPF/TVA code read via Jurisite excerpts and blogs) — attested, not exhaustively sourced.
